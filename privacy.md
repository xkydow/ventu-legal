# Dipp Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026
**Last updated:** October 15, 2026

Dipp ("Dipp", "we", "us") is a mobile dating app that helps adults meet in person. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Dipp is operated by Felix Ernesto Suarez Aspiazu, an individual in Miami-Dade County, Florida, United States. Dipp was previously called Ventu.

If you have questions, write to **xkydow@icloud.com**.

## 1. Who can use Dipp
Dipp is only for people who are **18 or older**. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.

## 2. Information we collect

### Information you give us
- **Account information:** your phone number (to sign in with a text-message code), and your email address if you sign in with Apple or add a recovery email.
- **Profile information:** first name, date of birth (we show your age, not the date), gender, who you want to meet, languages you speak, what you're looking for, the kinds of plans you like, whether you drink alcohol, whether you have children, your weekly availability, a short bio or daily status, and 2 to 3 photos.
- **Voice note:** if you record one, a short audio clip on your profile.
- **Verification selfie:** a live selfie to confirm you're the person in your photos (see "Selfie verification" below).
- **Messages:** what you write to your matches in the chat.
- **Date plans:** the invitations you send or accept, the times and places chosen, and whether the date was confirmed or cancelled.
- **Reports and blocks:** reports you send about other people and the people you block.
- **Support:** anything you send us when you contact us.

Some of this information may be considered sensitive in some places, such as your gender, who you want to meet (which can reveal your sexual orientation) and your selfie (see "Selfie verification" below). You choose to give it to us so Dipp can show you compatible people. You can change it in your profile at any time.

### Information we collect when you use the app
- **Location:** with your permission, your device's precise location **while you use the app**. We use it to show people near you, sort them by distance, and suggest places for your date. **We never show your exact location to other people**; they see only an approximate distance in miles. You can turn location off in your iPhone settings, but Discover needs it to work.
- **Device and technical information:** app version, device model, operating system, language, and error reports, so the app works and we can fix problems.
- **Usage information:** actions in the app, like the profiles you invite, so the app can work (for example, not showing you the same person twice), and which screens are opened, to understand how the app is used in aggregate.

### Information from others
- **Sign in with Apple:** your name and email (which may be a private relay address from Apple), if you choose to share them.
- **Other users:** for example, when someone reports you or sends you an invitation.

## 3. How we use your information
- To create and run your account and verify your phone number.
- To show your profile to other users and show you people who match what you're looking for.
- To help you plan dates: suggest times and places and open the chat when you match.
- To protect you before you meet: until after your first date, our server automatically hides phone numbers and social media usernames written in the chat (they appear as *******). This is done by software; nobody at Dipp reads your chat for it.
- To keep Dipp safe: verify with a selfie that you're the person in your photos, check profile photos for nudity, explicit content and graphic violence (see "Photo review" below), review reports, prevent fraud, spam and abuse, and enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines.
- To send you messages about your account and dates (for example, text-message codes and notifications you allow).
- To power Dipp AI, the in-app assistant that is part of Dipp (see "Dipp AI" below).
- To fix errors and improve the app.
- To comply with the law.

We **do not sell** your personal information, we **do not share it for targeted advertising**, and we **do not track you** across other companies' apps or websites.

## 4. What other users can see
Other users can see your profile: first name, age, photos, general area, approximate distance, languages, what you're looking for, the plans you like, whether you have children, your status, your voice note and your availability. Your matches can also see the messages you send them. Other users **cannot** see your phone number, email, date of birth, exact location, verification selfie, your chat with Dipp AI, or whether you drink alcohol.

## 5. Who we share information with
We share information only as needed to run Dipp:
- **Service providers** that work for us under contract: Supabase (database, storage and sign-in), Twilio (text-message codes), Apple (Sign in with Apple and notifications), Expo (app updates and notifications), Anthropic (the AI model behind Dipp AI), Amazon Web Services (the face comparison for selfie verification and the automatic review of profile photos), Sentry (crash and error reports) and PostHog (which screens are used, to improve the app). Sentry and PostHog only receive your account's random ID, never your name, phone, email, location, photos or messages. 
- **Legal and safety reasons:** if the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the safety of anyone.
- **Scam warnings:** if we remove someone for fraud or a scam, we may tell the people who talked to them, including that person's first name or profile identifier, so they can protect themselves.
- **Business transfers:** if Dipp is sold or merged, your information may pass to the new owner under this policy.

### Dipp AI (AI assistant)
Dipp AI is the chat assistant built into Dipp: it suggests people, plans and places for your dates. It uses an artificial intelligence model from **Anthropic**, a third-party AI provider. Because it is part of how Dipp works, **you must accept Dipp AI when you sign up to use Dipp**; we ask for your explicit permission with a checkbox on the welcome screen, before you sign up with Apple or your phone number (the checkbox links to the full notice of what is sent), and without it no account is created (accounts created before this are asked the next time they open the app), and nothing about you is sent to Anthropic before you accept. If you don't agree, you can sign out or delete your account. Our server sends Anthropic:
- the messages you write to Dipp AI;
- some of your information so the replies fit you: your first name, age, what you're looking for, the plans you like, your interests, whether you're going out today, your dates (the other person's first name, and the day, time and place) and your own opinion of each date (whether you liked it, whether you'd repeat, and up to 3 fixed tags), and the plans and interests most common among the people you sent a Go Out! to (never who they are);
- Dipp AI's private notes about your tastes (see "Dipp AI's private notes" below); and
- to suggest people for you, the same information about other members who use Dipp AI (first name, age, what they're looking for, plans, interests) and whether they're going out today.

This means that **when you accept Dipp AI, your first name, age, what you're looking for, plans, interests and whether you're going out today may be sent to Anthropic, our AI provider, so Dipp AI can suggest you to other members who use Dipp AI.** Only members who have accepted Dipp AI are suggested to others. This is the same information those members can already see on your profile. And **when someone has a date with you, your first name and the date's details (day, time and place) may be sent to Anthropic** so Dipp AI can help that person with the date. If you send them a whisper (a short line Dipp AI passes on before the date), it also reaches their chat with Dipp AI.

We **never** send anyone's phone number, email or location to Anthropic. Anthropic processes this information only to generate Dipp AI's replies for us, under its terms for business customers, and does not use it to train its models.

**Dipp AI's private notes.** So its suggestions get better over time, Dipp AI keeps short private notes about your tastes, written by the AI from your chat with Dipp AI and the signals above: for example, the plans and places you enjoy, the days you usually go out and how your past dates went. The notes are limited to about 1,200 characters, are written without sensitive details (such as health, religion, politics, race, money or other people's names), and are used only to personalize Dipp AI for you. Nobody sees them in the app, not other members and not you, and they are never shown on your profile. You can ask us for a copy of your notes at any time by writing to xkydow@icloud.com.

**Your opinion of your dates.** After a date, Dipp AI can save your opinion of it: whether you liked it, whether you'd repeat, and up to 3 tags from a fixed list (such as "on time", "good conversation" or "good place"). We never save free text about the other person. Only you see your opinion; the other person and other members never do. It is used only to personalize Dipp AI for you, and it is deleted with "Reset what Dipp knows about you", when the date or your account is deleted, and in any case after 12 months.

Your chat with Dipp AI is private: only you see it. You can delete your chat, Dipp AI's private notes and your opinion of your dates at any time with **"Reset what Dipp knows about you"** in Settings → Privacy & Safety. To withdraw your permission for Dipp AI completely, delete your account from **Settings → Delete account**: from then on nothing about you is sent to Anthropic.

### Selfie verification (biometric information)
To help keep Dipp safe, we ask you to take a live selfie to confirm that you are the person in your profile photos. This section is our written policy on how we collect, use, keep and destroy biometric information.
- **What we collect:** the selfie you take in the app. To compare it with your profile photos, a facial comparison creates measurements of your face (facial geometry). Some laws treat this as **biometric information**.
- **How it's used:** only to check that your selfie matches your profile photos. We send the selfie and your profile photos to **Amazon Web Services (Amazon Rekognition)**, which compares them for us and returns a similarity score. Amazon processes them only for this comparison, on our behalf, and does not keep the facial measurements after the comparison, and does not use your photos to improve or train its own services.
- **Human review:** if the automatic check fails or is unclear, a member of the Dipp team may look at the selfie and your photos to decide.
- **How long we keep it:** we delete the selfie as soon as the check passes or our team makes a decision. If no decision is made within 7 days, we delete it anyway. If there is an open safety report or a suspension under review on the account, we may keep it until the case is closed, but **never for more than 90 days** after you took it. The only exception is if a court order, subpoena or law enforcement request requires us to preserve it; then we keep only what is required, only for as long as it is required. We keep only the result (verified or not). Everything is also deleted if you delete your account.
- **Never:** we don't sell, rent, trade or share your biometric information for advertising, and we don't use it to identify you anywhere else.
- **Your consent:** we ask for your permission on the selfie screen before taking the selfie. Selfie verification is required to send or accept a date. If you don't agree, don't take the selfie: you can keep your profile and use the rest of Dipp, but you can't plan dates.

### Photo review
To keep Dipp free of nudity, explicit content and graphic violence, every profile photo you upload is checked automatically shortly after you upload it.
- **How it works:** we send the photo to **Amazon Web Services (Amazon Rekognition)**, which looks for nudity, explicit content or graphic violence and tells us what it found. It does not identify who is in the photo, and it doesn't create measurements of your face. Amazon processes the photo only to run this check, on our behalf, and does not use it to improve or train its own services.
- **What happens next:** if the photo is explicit, or the result is unclear, it is taken off your profile right away and a member of the Dipp team looks at it and decides. If the team keeps it, it goes back on your profile; if the team removes it, we delete the file. We let you know either way. If the automatic check can't run, the photo stays up and our team reviews it.
- **What we keep:** the result of the check (what was found and how sure the check was). A photo waiting for review is kept privately until the team decides.

## 6. How long we keep information
We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account from **Settings → Delete account**, we permanently delete your profile, photos, voice note, selfie, location, availability, messages, date plans, your chat with Dipp AI, Dipp AI's private notes about you and your opinion of your dates. Your opinion of each date is deleted after 12 months even while your account is open. We may keep a limited record for up to 2 years (or longer if the law requires) of safety actions (like suspensions) to stop people who broke our rules from coming back, and backups are erased on their normal cycle within 30 days.

## 7. Your choices and rights
- **Edit** your profile at any time in the app.
- **Location, camera, photos, microphone, calendar and notifications:** turn them on or off in your iPhone settings.
- **Dipp AI:** use "Reset what Dipp knows about you" in Settings → Privacy & Safety to delete your chat and Dipp AI's private notes. To withdraw your permission for Dipp AI, delete your account. To get a copy of the notes first, write to xkydow@icloud.com.
- **Block and report** people from their profile.
- **Delete your account** in Settings → Delete account.
- **Access or copy** your information, including Dipp AI's private notes about you: write to xkydow@icloud.com.

Wherever you live in the United States, you can ask us to tell you what we have about you, give you a copy, correct it or delete it, and we'll answer within 45 days. Depending on your state, you may have additional rights under its privacy law. To use any of these rights, write to xkydow@icloud.com from the email on your account or tell us the phone number on your account, so we can confirm it's you. If we say no, you can write back to ask us to reconsider. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

## 8. Security
We use reasonable measures to protect your information, such as encryption in transit, access controls and database rules that limit who can read each piece of data. No system is 100% secure, so please use Dipp carefully and never share passwords or codes.
