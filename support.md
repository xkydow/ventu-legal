# Dipp Support

Need help with Dipp? Write to us at **xkydow@icloud.com**. We reply by email. In the app you can also go to **Settings → Help & Contact** to email us or report a problem.

**If you are in danger, call 911.** Dipp can't send help.

## Safety
- **Report or block someone:** on their profile tap **See all → Report or block**, or use the chat menu. They won't know you reported them. You can unblock in Settings → Blocked Users.
- To tell us about a safety problem on a date, write to us with the person's name in the app and what happened. We review every report.

## Your account
- **Delete your account:** **Settings → Delete Account**. Your profile, photos, matches, dates and messages are deleted for good, as explained in our [Privacy Policy](./privacy). It can't be undone.
- **Change your phone number or recovery email:** Settings → Account & ID.
- **Ask for a copy of your information** or anything else about your privacy: write to us from the email on your account, or tell us the phone number on your account. Sharing your account ID (Settings → Account & ID) helps us find you faster.

## Beta testers
Thanks for testing Dipp! Send feedback from TestFlight (take a screenshot, then tap Share Beta Feedback) or write to us at the email above.
