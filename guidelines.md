# Ventu Community Guidelines

Ventu is for adults who want to meet in person. These rules keep it safe and respectful, and they apply both in the app and on your dates. Breaking them, on or off Ventu, can get your content removed or your account suspended or banned, and we may report serious cases to the police.

## Be yourself
- Use your real first name and your real age.
- Use recent photos where your face is clearly visible. No photos of other people, celebrities, cartoons or AI-generated faces.
- One account per person.

## Be respectful
- No harassment, bullying, threats, or unwanted sexual messages.
- No hate speech: no attacks on anyone because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.
- "No" means no. If someone doesn't reply, cancels or blocks you, let it go.

## Keep it safe and legal
- 18+ only. Anything involving minors is reported to the authorities.
- No nudity or sexual content in photos, voice notes or statuses.
- No violence, weapons or illegal drugs.
- No sex work, escorting or "sugar" arrangements.
- No scams: never ask for money, gift cards, crypto or financial information.
- No selling, advertising, promotions, or links to other accounts to gain followers.
- Don't share someone else's private information or screenshots of their messages without permission.

## Show up
Ventu is about real dates. If you confirm a date, go, or cancel with as much notice as you can.

## Meet safely
- Meet in a public place. Ventu suggests places for that reason.
- Tell a friend where you're going and who you're meeting.
- Arrange your own ride and stay in control of your drink.
- If something feels wrong, leave. **If you are in danger, call 911.**

## Report and block
If someone breaks these rules or makes you feel unsafe, report them from their profile (at the bottom of "See all") or block them. Reports are confidential: the other person isn't told who reported them.
